DualTimemark · iPhone

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the iPhone app and this support website.

Overview

DualTimemark is an iPhone camera application for combining supported camera views and adding customizable watermarks. No app account is required.

Camera, microphone, and photos

Camera input is processed on your iPhone to create photos and videos. The microphone records sound for videos when permitted. Saved results go to your Photos library. Optional Photos access lets the app display the latest photo thumbnail. The reviewed app code does not upload captured photos or videos to a developer server. Your device’s iCloud Photos and backup settings may separately sync saved media through Apple.

Location and weather

With your permission, the app uses current coordinates, altitude and heading for location-related watermarks. Apple location, geocoding and map services provide addresses, nearby places and maps. The app requests current weather directly through Apple WeatherKit for your location. The app does not send weather coordinates to a developer-operated server or create developer server logs. Apple processes requests under its applicable privacy terms. If weather is unavailable, including service quota or request failures, weather details are not displayed.

Local storage

Camera settings, watermark preferences, custom fields, selected locations and recent location-search cache may be stored locally. Captured media remains in Photos until you delete it there. Removing the app does not delete media already saved to Photos.

Choices

You may deny or revoke optional Location access in iOS Settings. This disables automatically obtained location/weather details without removing basic capture functionality. Camera access is needed for capture; microphone access is requested for video sound. Photos permissions control saving and thumbnail access. You can edit watermark fields, and should review location information before sharing a photo or video.

Advertising and tracking

The reviewed build contains no advertising SDK, tracking code, sign-in system, or in-app payment flow. We do not use the app’s data for cross-app advertising tracking.

Contact and rights

For privacy questions or requests, contact waynelu1981@gmail.com. You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings, remove locally stored app settings by deleting the app, and delete captured media in Photos. We do not maintain an app account or developer-server copy of your captured media or weather coordinates.

Changes

The published policy will include its effective date. Material changes to processing practices will be reflected in an updated policy.

Support communications

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to provide are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending private photos, precise locations or other unnecessary sensitive information. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations.
