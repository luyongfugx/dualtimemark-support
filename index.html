DualTimemark · iPhone

Dual-camera capture. Your details, in frame.

DualTimemark combines supported front and rear camera views with multiple customizable watermark styles. Choose picture-in-picture, top/bottom or side-by-side layouts for photos and videos.

iPhone only · iOS 26 or later
Simultaneous capture requires compatible multicamera hardware. No account is required.

How do I change the layout?

Tap the layout icon on the right side of the camera and choose Picture in picture, Top and bottom, Side by side, or Single.

How do I change the watermark?

Tap the bottom-right watermark button, choose a template, then use Edit watermark to customize the available fields.

Where are my photos and videos?

After saving completes, open the Photos app. Make sure the app has permission to add media.

Why are location, weather or maps missing?

Check Location permission and network access. Availability varies by service and area. Location access is optional.

Why can’t two lenses work together?

Not every device or lens pair supports simultaneous capture. Select an available combination; stop recording before changing lenses.

How do I change the language?

Open Camera settings, then Language. The app offers English, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, and Indonesian.
